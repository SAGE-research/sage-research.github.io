About SAGE Research Lab

Welcome to the SAGE Research Lab — Security, AI, and Generative systems Education Lab — at the School of Informatics, Computing, and Cyber Systems (SICCS), Northern Arizona University.

Our mission is twofold: we secure AI-driven systems against a broad spectrum of threats — adversarial attacks on ML models, prompt injection and jailbreaks targeting large language models, logic vulnerabilities in firmware and IoT platforms, privacy leakage, and network intrusions — and we harness AI to advance cybersecurity education. SAGE Research Lab pushes the frontier where artificial intelligence and security meet.

We are actively looking for motivated Ph.D. students and research collaborators. If you are interested in joining, please reach out to Dr. Lan Zhang with your CV and a brief research statement.

Research Areas

Our lab investigates the security of AI-driven systems and builds defenses against emerging threats — from adversarial machine learning to LLM jailbreaks, firmware vulnerabilities, and the use of LLMs to advance security education.

AI / ML Security

Adversarial attacks and defenses for ML-based malware detectors and graph neural networks; semantics-preserving reinforcement learning attacks; and bias analysis in deep learning classifiers.

LLM Security

Prompt injection in multimodal LLMs, jailbreaking via neural carrier articles, LLM fingerprinting, and harnessing LLMs for automated vulnerability repair and code security analysis.

Firmware & IoT Security

Specification-guided firmware emulation (ACM CCS'22), logic bug discovery in IoT platforms, and context-aware protocol fuzzing for Bluetooth Low Energy stacks (NDSS'26).

Network Intrusion Detection

Neural network-based detection of network attacks, explainable AI for intrusion analysis, and evaluation of deep learning detectors across diverse attack scenarios.

Privacy & Trustworthy ML

Membership inference attacks and defenses, privacy-preserving machine learning under homomorphic encryption, and federated learning for sensitive domains such as healthcare.

LLM in Security Education

Leveraging large language models to enhance cybersecurity education — automated CTI operationalization, AI-assisted vulnerability explanation, and intelligent tutoring for security concepts.

People

Lan Zhang
Assistant Professor
School of Informatics, Computing, and Cyber Systems (SICCS), NAU

Dr. Lan Zhang's research lies at the intersection of cybersecurity and machine learning. She works on securing AI/ML systems against adversarial attacks, hardening large language models against prompt injection and jailbreaks, exposing logic vulnerabilities in firmware and IoT platforms, and leveraging LLMs to advance cybersecurity education. Her work has appeared at top-tier venues including ACM CCS, NDSS, IEEE DSN, IEEE TDSC, and IEEE TIFS.
